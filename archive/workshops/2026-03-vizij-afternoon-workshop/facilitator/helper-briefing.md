# Helper Briefing

Last updated: 2026-03-13

Use this for the 20 to 30 minute helper sync before the event.

## Workshop Operating Model

The room model for this tutorial is:

1. facilitator-led,
2. coding-capable,
3. pair-based,
4. mixed readiness across attendee machines,
5. facilitator demo as the canonical path if participant laptops fail.

The goal is to keep the whole room moving, not to debug every machine to perfection.

## Helper Roles

Minimum helper assignment:

1. Helper 1: install and environment lane
2. Helper 2: authoring and runtime-control lane during Activity 1
3. Helper 3: animation, integration, and deployment lane during Activity 2

If a fourth helper is available:

1. make them a floater during activities,
2. give them breakout note-capture responsibility starting at 4:30 pm.

## What Helpers Should Watch For

### Introduction

1. Identify who is ready to run apps and who needs setup help.
2. Move immediately toward the setup cluster while the facilitator continues presenting.

### Activity 1

1. Keep participants on the current step instead of letting them wander ahead.
2. Prioritize:
   - app launch failures,
   - sample asset confusion,
   - path-model confusion,
   - participants who cannot tell the difference between standard and pose-weight paths.

### First Support Window

Operate two visible lanes:

1. Lane A: setup and app-launch recovery
2. Lane B: path-model and authoring exploration

If a participant is unblocked, move them into one exploration task rather than leaving them idle.

### Activity 2

1. Treat `tutorial-agent-face` as facilitator-led only.
2. Help participants with `demo-vizij-player`.
3. Treat `vizij-standalone` as guided architecture plus control-path understanding, not a participant build-and-ship exercise.

### Second Support Window

Prioritize:

1. anyone who never completed Activity 1,
2. anyone who cannot distinguish animation from live control,
3. anyone blocked on deployment/control-path mental model.

Reserve at least one helper to begin breakout setup.

## Fallback Rule

If a participant machine fails:

1. the facilitator machine becomes the canonical reference path,
2. the participant continues with the worksheet and partner observation,
3. helpers keep the participant learning the model even if local execution is stalled.

## Breakout Hour

At 5:00 pm:

1. split the room into roadmap/product, research/technical, and deployment/adoption groups,
2. assign one reporter and one note-taker per group,
3. keep each group focused on three outputs:
   - one prioritized opportunity
   - one blocker
   - one next experiment or milestone

At 5:40 pm:

1. call groups back,
2. make sure each group has its three outputs ready for report-back.
