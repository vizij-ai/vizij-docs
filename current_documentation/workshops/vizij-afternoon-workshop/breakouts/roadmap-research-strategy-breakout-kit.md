# Roadmap, Research, and Strategy Breakout Kit

Last updated: 2026-03-09

Time block: `5:00 pm to 6:00 pm`

Primary source:

1. `vizij-docs/current_documentation/ROADMAP.md`

## Purpose

Convert the day’s hands-on experience into actionable product and research feedback.

## Group Structure

Create three groups:

1. Product and roadmap priorities
2. Research and technical opportunities
3. Deployment, adoption, and integration risks

Each group should assign:

1. one facilitator
2. one note-taker
3. one reporter

## Prompt Set

### Group 1: Product and Roadmap Priorities

1. Which workflow felt most ready for broader use?
2. Which missing feature most limits workshop or user success?
3. Which near-term roadmap item would create the most leverage?

### Group 2: Research and Technical Opportunities

1. Which runtime or authoring concept deserves deeper research?
2. Which technical gap could unlock a new class of faces or integrations?
3. What experiment would best validate that opportunity?

### Group 3: Deployment and Adoption Risks

1. What stands between a demo and a real robot-facing deployment?
2. Which operational or integration risks need to be addressed first?
3. What packaging, docs, or tooling change would reduce adoption friction?

## Expected Output

Each group should return:

1. one prioritized opportunity
2. one blocker or risk
3. one suggested next experiment or milestone

## Facilitator Notes

1. Keep groups focused on concrete outcomes, not vague reactions.
2. Ask groups to tie every claim to something they saw in the workshop.
3. Use the report-back template so outputs are comparable.
