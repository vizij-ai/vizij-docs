# Materials Walkthrough

Last updated: 2026-03-09

Open this file first when you are preparing to run the workshop.

## Before the Session

1. Read `checklists/day-of-facilitator-checklist.md`.
2. Open `facilitator/demo-command-sheet.md`.
3. Launch the needed apps from `vizij-web`.
4. Open the deck:
   - `slides/vizij-afternoon-workshop-deck.html` for live presentation
   - `slides/vizij-afternoon-workshop-deck.pdf` as the fallback
5. Keep `slides/speaker-notes.md` open on the presenter machine.
6. Print or share `participant/vizij-afternoon-workbook.pdf` if you want a single participant handout.

## During the Introduction

Use:

1. `slides/vizij-afternoon-workshop-deck.html`
2. `facilitator/run-of-show.md`
3. `facilitator/facilitator-guide.md`

Goal:

1. Set expectations, show the teaser, and make the room comfortable with the hands-on flow.

## During Activity 1

Use:

1. `participant/participant-guide.md`
2. `participant/vizij-afternoon-workbook.pdf`
3. `exercises/activity-1-face-design-control.md`
4. `handouts/vizij-runtime-quickstart.md`
5. `handouts/renderer-rigging-reference.md`

Goal:

1. Move participants from inspecting a face to controlling a face and reasoning about the path model.

## During Support Windows

Use:

1. `checklists/troubleshooting-playbook.md`
2. `exercises/support-and-exploration-menu.md`

Goal:

1. Keep blocked participants moving without slowing the entire room.

## During Activity 2

Use:

1. `exercises/activity-2-animation-integration-deployment.md`
2. `handouts/animation-integration-deployment-reference.md`
3. `facilitator/facilitator-guide.md`

Goal:

1. Connect animation, live integration, and deployment into one understandable story.

## During the Breakout Hour

Use:

1. `breakouts/roadmap-research-strategy-breakout-kit.md`
2. `breakouts/report-back-template.md`
3. `vizij-docs/current_documentation/ROADMAP.md`

Goal:

1. Turn the workshop experience into structured roadmap and strategy feedback.

## If Something Fails

Use:

1. `checklists/troubleshooting-playbook.md`
2. the fallback guidance in `facilitator/facilitator-guide.md`

Rule:

1. Keep teaching the model even if one live integration is unavailable.
